03. Proprietary Fit Architecture

Proprietary ingestion. Deterministic geometry.

Generic AI wrappers guess sizes and hallucinate numbers. Pearfit is built end-to-end on our proprietary review-scraping parser and deterministic geometric engine—extracting fit signals and body-zone ease without relying on third-party LLMs. Every answer is fast, repeatable, and 100% traceable to its underlying measurements.

  1. 01. Ingest

    Proprietary catalog & review parser

    Our purpose-built scraper and lexical parser index your product specs and review feeds (Okendo, Yotpo, Judge.me, Junip) to classify per-size fit and body-zone signals.

  2. 02. Normalize

    Unify every measurement

    Charts, flat-lay specs, and reference garments map into a single centimeter schema with provenance tags on every dimension.

  3. 03. Compare

    Score point-by-point ease

    Our geometric engine weights critical anchor points—like shoulder yoke and chest sweep on woven tops—against the shopper’s reference garment.

  4. 04. Calibrate

    Close the loop on returns

    Verified keep, exchange, and return outcomes automatically tune category weights and grading thresholds for your specific catalog.

Zero-Hallucination Guarantee: Because Pearfit runs on a deterministic proprietary engine rather than generative AI, missing measurements stay missing—we never invent a number, and we adjust the confidence score transparently whenever a spec table is incomplete.

07. Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for brands and partners

What data does Pearfit need to launch a pilot?

Only your product page URLs, existing garment size charts or tech packs, and read access to your customer reviews. Even when flat-lay measurements are incomplete, our proprietary review-scraping engine immediately powers Fit Insights.

How do shoppers tell Pearfit what clothes they already own?

Shoppers select a staple garment they already own and love the fit of—by brand, style, and size—from our indexed database of 4,200+ reference items, or enter three quick flat measurements. Pearfit compares every size of your SKU against that benchmark.

How does Pearfit’s proprietary engine work without LLMs?

Generic LLM wrappers are slow, expensive, and prone to hallucinating measurements. Pearfit uses our own purpose-built review scraper, domain-specific lexical parser, and deterministic geometric engine to extract per-size sentiment and compute centimeter ease—so every recommendation is fast, repeatable, and 100% explainable to both shoppers and your CX team.

How do you measure ROI during the 6-week pilot?

We establish a pre-pilot baseline of size/fit return rates and multi-size bracketing on your target category, then compare cohorts who interacted with the Pearfit Size Advisor against holdout traffic over 6 weeks.

How is shopper privacy handled?

Pearfit never requires body photos or webcam scans. We store only anonymous reference-garment preferences so returning shoppers get instant recommendations across any Pearfit-enabled storefront.
